# Code of Conduct

Open Commerce follows a contributor covenant style standard: contributors should keep discussion respectful, specific, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and implementation constraints.
- Give technical feedback with enough detail to be actionable.
- Keep commercial claims, unverifiable data, and private information out of public issues and pull requests.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private or sensitive information.
- Misrepresenting unofficial, inferred, or estimated data as verified official data.
- Repeatedly disrupting issue, pull request, or discussion workflows.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the project or community.

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).
